Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed version of your password. We never store your plain text password. When you search, we log the search query and timestamp associated with your account. We collect basic usage data such as which features you use and how often.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, address, or any payment card details. Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your card number.

How we use your data

We use your email to identify your account and send you transactional emails such as password resets. We use your search history to display it back to you in the app. We use aggregate usage data to improve the product.

What we never do

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. This is a founding principle of FlipLens, not a policy subject to change without notice.

Third party services

FlipLens uses Stripe for payment processing and Supabase for database hosting. Both are reputable services with their own privacy policies. We use official APIs from Discogs, Reverb, Etsy, JustTCG, and eBay to fetch pricing data — we do not share your data with these services beyond what is necessary to perform a search.

Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be deleted within 30 days. Search history and cached results may be retained in anonymized form for product improvement.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account at any time by contacting us. We will respond within 30 days.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@fliplens.com.
